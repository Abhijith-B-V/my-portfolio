About

I treat trust as a feature — not something you assume about your data, but something you build and prove.

Every project I ship shares one rule — the result is measured against ground truth, so "it works" is a number I can defend, not a claim I'm hoping holds. I'm currently completing an M.S. in Applied Information Technology at George Mason University.
